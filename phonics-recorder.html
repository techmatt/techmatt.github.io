Phonics Sound Recorder

12 short takes for a children's reading game. Your recordings stay on this computer until you send the zip.

How to record (read once)
  • Each take is one card of three: read the word, then say its sound three times, with a short pause (about a second) between everything. The sound to say is the red part of the word. For example: “VAN … v … v … v … CAN … c … c … c …”.
  • Hold sounds are stretched: “sssss”, “mmmmm”, “aaaa”, about a second each.
  • Short sounds are crisp, with no “uh” after them: /c/ not “kuh”, /b/ not “buh”. Whisper-short is fine.
  • Vowels are the short sound in the word: A as in ANT, E as in HEN, I as in PIN, O as in FOX, U as in BUG.
  • A quiet room, about a hand's width from the mic. Press Record (or Space), read the card, press Stop. Your first take of a card saves itself. A later take plays back beside the saved one; press Keep new if it's better.
  • When you're done (or any time), press Download zip and send that file back.
